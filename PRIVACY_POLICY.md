# Privacy Policy for Tic Tac Toe

**Last updated:** February 24, 2026

## About Tic Tac Toe

**Tic Tac Toe** is a modern, futuristic neon-themed strategy puzzle game developed by **Palankar.R**. The game features multiple gameplay modes including single-player vs AI (Easy, Medium, and Hard difficulty levels) and local two-player pass-and-play mode across classic 3x3, 6x6, 9x9, and 11x11 board configurations.

This game is provided at no cost and is intended for use as is. To keep the app completely free for all players, it incorporates non-intrusive advertisements (Google AdMob) and basic performance analytics (Firebase Analytics).
