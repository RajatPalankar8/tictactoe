Privacy Policy for Tic Tac Toe

Last updated: February 24, 2026

About Tic Tac Toe

Tic Tac Toe is a modern, futuristic neon-themed strategy puzzle game developed by Palankar.R. The game offers single-player matches against smart AI levels (Easy, Medium, Hard) and local two-player modes across multiple board grid sizes (3x3 Classic, 6x6 Mega, 9x9 Pro, and 11x11 Ultimate).

This game is provided at no cost and is intended for use as is. To keep the app completely free for all players, it incorporates non-intrusive advertisements (Google AdMob) and basic performance analytics (Firebase Analytics).

Privacy Policy Terms

This page is used to inform players regarding our policies with the collection, use, and disclosure of Personal Information for anyone who decides to use our Service.

1. Information Collection and Use

For a better experience while playing Tic Tac Toe, we do not require you to provide any personally identifiable information (such as your name, email address, or phone number). The information requested remains on your device and is not collected by us.

Third-Party Service Providers

The app utilizes trusted third-party services that may collect non-personally identifiable device information:

  • Google Play Services

2. Advertising & Analytics

Our game is free to play because it displays ads powered by Google AdMob (banner and interstitial ads). These services may use anonymous Advertising IDs provided by your device to deliver relevant ads. Additionally, Firebase Analytics collects anonymous diagnostic data to help us improve performance and stability.

3. Children’s Privacy

These Services do not address anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13 years of age.

4. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. You are advised to review this page periodically for any changes.
